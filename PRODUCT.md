# Health
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
android
## Users and purpose
Russian-speaking student wants one independent APK for Android 16. Phone tracks runs, walks, distance, routes, steps and sleep estimates. Optional direct Mi Band 7 (non-Pro) integration.
## Brand commitments
Material You 3. Google Sans. Dynamic colors, light and dark themes.
## Constraints
Real measurements only. No fabricated health readings. Phone cannot identify clinical sleep stages. Mi Band needs a private pairing key; hardware integration must be tested on user's device.
