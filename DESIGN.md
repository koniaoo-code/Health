# Health interface

Version 1.1 keeps the fully Kotlin codebase and the adaptive Material You 3 launcher icon (green #36693E base, white pulse, monochrome layer for themed icons). Version 0.3 introduced native Material 3 surfaces with wallpaper-derived dynamic colors and system, light, and dark themes. Google Sans is bundled with its OFL license. Four bottom destinations organize Today, Activity, Sleep, and Food. Band access and secondary tools live in the scrollable profile sheet. The header opens global history. Russian section headings, rounded tonal surfaces, compact lists, wrapping chips, and pill actions separate summaries from controls. Main screens use 20 dp horizontal padding, 24 dp card corners, 32 sp headings, and 16 sp row titles; the header history and profile buttons are fixed at exactly 48 dp so both circles render equal on every theme.

Today shows a live phone step total with a configurable goal and a linear progress indicator, plus the water line with the percent of the daily norm. A home-screen widget mirrors the daily count and goal progress, and the step service notification reports the same progress. The water sheet contains an animated glass model (decaying wave surface, rising bubbles, smooth fill toward the daily norm of 2000 ml), icon chips for cup/glass/mug/bottle portions, free numeric input, and a removal action; the sheet updates totals live and re-renders the page on dismiss. Activity and Sleep show recent sessions with access to full history. Food groups compact entries by meal, totals the day with the day-calorie card (estimate labeled), and places catalog search in a bottom sheet. Global history groups records by effective date, filters by date and type, exports and imports JSON (import merges by stable record IDs), and lists water and mood chips. Step totals and water events are read-only in history; water controls log the actual clamped change. Legacy preference histories migrate transactionally, retaining their original raw preferences.

Version 1.1 refines the honesty of sleep and food surfaces. Sleep subtitles read "rest ≈P% · calm night" style, where the word comes from the share of still minutes (≥70 «очень тихая ночь», ≥55 «спокойная ночь», ≥40 «ночь с перепадами», otherwise «беспокойная ночь»). The night sheet focuses on three rows (rest minutes, movement minutes, longest quiet stretch) and pushes raw sensor minutes and coverage gaps into footnotes; the deep-sleep estimate is explicitly labeled actigraphy, not clinical. Food entry adds an equal-height three-button S/M/L portion row (S ≈×0.7, M baseline, L ≈×1.5 of the catalog value) with a plain-language legend, the day card totals portion-size-aware calories labeled as an estimate, record subtitles replace the generic "plate" with readable portions, and each meal record shows how the calories were estimated (exact dish, full brand-key network portion, category average or home combo) plus the selected size. Steps render estimated kilometers — steps × (height × 0.414) ÷ 100 000 — in history, step details and weekly trends.

A Kotlin foreground step service persists its sensor baseline and attempts recovery after reboot. Workout step deltas do not write the daily total, and disabling the step service does not stop an active workout. Kotlin also implements the Bluetooth protocol (BandClient, ECDH_B163), sleep service, SQLite store, catalogs, graph views, and routes.

Workout routes show a schematic path and measured totals. Sleep screens describe accelerometer observation and screen-off estimates without claiming sleep stages or clinical sleep duration. The optional dim night clock uses subdued text to reduce bedroom light. Profile BMI remains numeric only, with no adult weight categories or weight goals. Material text fields retain labels after input. Main content and the profile sheet scroll and use system insets; interactive rows are at least 68 dp high, chips request a minimum touch target, and the S/M/L selector uses a fixed 52 dp height with only the letters on the buttons.

Build and Android instrumentation execution were pending through v0.3 review; no v0.3 screenshots had been reviewed at that time. For v1.1, `:app:assembleDebug` builds clean (versionCode 5, versionName 1.1.0), and a Pixel 6a emulator screenshot pass approved the Today screen (kilometers, water percent), sleep word subtitles, the food flow (category chips, S/M/L dialog, saved entry, day calorie card, record details with the estimation source), and the equal header circles. Lint and the HistoryCheck instrumentation were not rerun in 1.1. Physical GPS, screen-off accelerometer recording, background step reliability across device firmware, button-large-font and TalkBack behavior, and Mi Band 7 compatibility remain unverified. No web-interface detector applies to this native Android app.

0.3 also adds an isolated Map screen from Today and saved workout details: OpenStreetMap basemap, current-location marker, route bounds and explicit recenter control.
